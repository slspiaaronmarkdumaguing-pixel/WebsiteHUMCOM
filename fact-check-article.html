Myth

Sugar causes
immediate
diabetes.

Sources:

WHO | CDC | NIH

The truth: The claim that eating sugar causes immediate diabetes is a misconception. Consuming sugary foods or drinks does not automatically cause a person to develop diabetes. Diabetes is a medical condition that develops due to problems with how the body produces or uses insulin, a hormone responsible for regulating blood glucose levels.

There are different types of diabetes. Type 1 diabetes is an autoimmune condition in which the immune system attacks the insulin-producing cells in the pancreas. It is not caused by eating sugar. Meanwhile, Type 2 diabetes develops when the body becomes resistant to insulin or does not produce enough insulin to maintain normal blood glucose levels.

Its development is influenced by several factors, including genetics, family history, lifestyle, and other health conditions.
